## 1. What I Learned

During Week 01, I learned the differences between Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Deep Learning (DL), Generative AI, and AI agents. I learned that AI can help explain concepts and organize information, but its output is not always factually correct.

I also learned that language models generate responses using learned patterns. A fluent and confident answer should not automatically be treated as verified information.

## 2. Comparing AI Assistants

I compared ChatGPT and Gemini using the same prompt:

> Explain the difference between Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI to a beginner engineer, and provide an everyday example for each.

ChatGPT used a concentric-circles analogy to explain the relationships between the concepts. Gemini provided concise explanations and practical examples, including a distinction involving structured and unstructured data.

This comparison helped me understand that different AI assistants can explain the same topic in different ways. I should evaluate their clarity, limitations, and supporting evidence instead of relying only on how convincing an answer sounds.

## 3. Connecting AI Literacy to DFT

As a Design for Testability learner, I am interested in exploring how AI could assist semiconductor test engineering. Possible applications include organizing scan-test failure logs, summarizing recurring failure patterns, and helping engineers investigate test results.

These are potential applications rather than results demonstrated by this week's work. Any AI-generated suggestion about a test failure must be checked against actual test data, design information, and established engineering methods.

## 4. My Verification Approach

For important technical claims, I will:
1. Identify the exact claim made by the AI assistant.
2. Find a relevant and reliable technical source.
3. Compare the claim with the available evidence.
4. Record what the evidence supports and what remains uncertain.
5. Correct or qualify my notes when necessary.

## 5. What I Found Challenging

One challenge is determining whether a technical explanation is sufficiently supported by evidence, especially when I am learning a new topic. An explanation may sound reasonable but omit assumptions or limitations.

## 6. Question I Still Have

How could AI help identify patterns in scan-test failures without producing misleading conclusions about the root cause?

## 7. Plan for Week 02

I will continue developing my AI literacy and explore how AI tools can support DFT learning while keeping technical verification and engineering judgment central.
