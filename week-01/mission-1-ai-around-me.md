## Evidence check

A feature's behaviour alone does not prove that it uses AI. For a real submission, choose a service you personally use and consult that provider's official documentation. Record the page title, URL, the claim it supports, and the date you checked it.

**Evidence status for this draft:** These are common examples, not proof about a particular app or device used by the student.

## Could a rule-based program do something similar?

A simple rule-based program can block emails containing specific words or recommend videos from a manually written list. However, it may struggle with context and changing patterns. A machine-learning system can learn statistical patterns from examples, though its output still needs evaluation.

## Conclusion

Everyday digital systems can predict, classify, recognize, or recommend. I should not label a feature as AI only because it seems intelligent; I should check reliable evidence about how the particular system works.
