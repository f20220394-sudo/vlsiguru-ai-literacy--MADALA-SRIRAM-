# Mission 8 — Where Could AI Help My VLSI Track?

## Selected area: Design Verification (DV)

| Repetitive or difficult task | How AI might help | Why engineering judgment still matters |
|---|---|---|
| Reviewing large verification logs and grouping recurring failure messages | Help summarize logs, cluster similar messages, and prioritize likely duplicates for investigation | The engineer must inspect the original log, reproduce the failure, distinguish real bugs from testbench issues, and verify any suggested root cause |

## Conclusion

AI could help a DV engineer reduce time spent organizing large volumes of verification output, but it cannot replace knowledge of the design, testbench, specifications, and verification methodology. Any AI-generated diagnosis must be checked against the actual evidence.

## Scope note

This is a proposed use case, not a claim that a particular tool or project has already delivered measurable improvements.
