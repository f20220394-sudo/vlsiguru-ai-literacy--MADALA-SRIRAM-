# Mission 9 — My AI Working Agreement

1. **Verify important claims.** I will check important technical or factual statements against reliable documentation, textbooks, specifications, or reproducible tests rather than trusting confident wording.
2. **Protect confidential information.** I will not paste company, lab, customer, or project information into an AI service unless its use is explicitly permitted by the relevant policy and data-handling rules.
3. **Own the final result.** I am responsible for understanding, checking, and explaining anything I submit, even when AI helped me draft or debug it.
4. **Test code and technical suggestions.** I will run small experiments when practical, inspect errors, and record what the test does and does not establish.
5. **Be transparent and keep evidence.** I will record important prompts, sources, assumptions, corrections, and limitations when they matter to the task.

## Why verification is essential

A generated answer may sound fluent without being fully correct. My check of the claim that confident LLM wording guarantees accuracy showed why confidence is not evidence. I will therefore verify important claims and state uncertainty instead of presenting guesses as facts.
