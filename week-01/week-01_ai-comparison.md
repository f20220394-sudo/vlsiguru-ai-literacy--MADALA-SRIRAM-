# Week 01 AI Assistant Comparison

## Common Prompt
> "Explain the difference between Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Generative AI to a beginner engineer, and provide an everyday example for each."

## Tool 1: ChatGPT (GPT-4o)
* **Answer Summary:** Provided a clear nesting analogy (concentric circles) defining ML as pattern learning, DL as multi-layered neural networks, and GenAI as content creation.
* **Strengths:** Excellent formatting, intuitive analogies, very readable structure.
* **Weaknesses:** Highly high-level; omitted operational caveats.

## Tool 2: Gemini
* **Answer Summary:** Defined each term clearly, highlighting data requirements for DL vs traditional ML, with structured everyday examples.
* **Strengths:** Strong technical distinction regarding unstructured vs structured data; concise summary points.
* **Weaknesses:** Slightly denser text formatting.

## Authoritative Reference
* IBM Education & MIT Technology Review standard technical definitions for AI/ML hierarchy.

## Final Comparison

| Criteria | ChatGPT | Gemini |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Accuracy** | High | High |

## Lesson Learned
Comparing multiple AI models highlights structural consensus while exposing nuances in how concepts are presented. Using multiple models combined with primary references eliminates blind trust in any single AI provider.
