# Mission 5 — Can AI Be Confidently Wrong?

## Verification log

**Question asked:** “Does a fluent answer from an LLM guarantee that every factual claim is correct?”

**Example AI answer to evaluate:** “Yes. If a language model gives a fluent, confident answer, its facts are reliable.”

**Independent check:** This statement is too strong. Fluency and confidence of wording do not establish factual accuracy. OpenAI's documentation on how ChatGPT and foundation models are developed describes model generation; it should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every generated claim is correct. A practical check is to compare a specific answer with a trustworthy primary source.

**Source to review:** OpenAI, “How ChatGPT and our foundation models are developed” — https://openai.com/index/how-chatgpt-and-our-foundation-models-are-developed/

**Result:** Incorrect as a general rule / unsupported claim.

**What the check proves:** It shows that confident wording alone is not a sufficient verification method and that claims need independent evidence.

**What the check does not prove:** It does not show that every LLM answer is wrong, or estimate the accuracy of all models across every task.

## Lesson

I should verify important factual and technical claims using authoritative documentation, reproducible tests, or other suitable evidence. I should record what the check supports and what remains uncertain.
